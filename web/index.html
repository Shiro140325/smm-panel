04 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an SMM panel?

An SMM (social media marketing) panel is a site where you buy followers, likes, views and other engagement for your social media pages. SMM Shiro is an SMM panel for the Philippines: every service shows its quality, start time, drop risk and refill terms, and you top up in pesos by QR Ph.

How do I pay?

Top up your wallet by QR Ph on PayMongo's secure checkout: scan the code with GCash, Maya or your bank app. Paying from the same phone? Save the QR image, then upload it in your app's scan screen. Your balance updates automatically once the payment is confirmed.

What happens if my order doesn't fully deliver?

If an order ends up partial or canceled, the undelivered part is refunded to your wallet balance automatically.

What does refill mean?

Some services lose part of what was delivered over time. On services with refill, you can request a top-up of what dropped from your Orders page, within the refill period after the order completes.

Do you need my password?

Never. We only need the public link to your profile, post or video. Keep your account public while the order runs.

How long until my order starts?

Each service shows its usual start time before you order. Most start within minutes to a few hours.
